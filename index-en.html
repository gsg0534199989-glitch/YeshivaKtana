About the Yeshiva

The new Zera Yitzchak Yeshiva was established by Rabbi Chaim Moshe Goldstein Shlit"a and his colleague Rabbi Dov Rodnick Shlit"a, with the encouragement and advice of HaGaon HaGadol Rabbi Yonason David Shlit"a, Rosh Yeshiva of Pachad Yitzchak, and under the personal guidance of his student, HaGaon Rav Chaim Yitzchak Kaplan Shlit"a, Mashgiach of Yeshivas Chevron.
The Yeshiva aims for the growth of the students in Torah with depth and honest deliberation, with a special emphasis on character traits and interpersonal relationships, in a wholesome and constructive yeshiva atmosphere.
HaGaon Rav Avraham Goldstein, Ram in Yeshivas HaTefutzot, Dayan, and Moreh Tzedek in Har Tzion, accompanies the Yeshiva daily by delivering a Shiur to all the students, with special emphasis on the depth of understanding in Jewish Law (Halacha), and once a week in the evenings with a Mussar talk on the greatness of man and perspective on current affairs and events.
